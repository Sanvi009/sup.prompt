When you create an account on spairogram, we collect the following personal information:

You may also choose to provide additional information in your profile, but we do not require it.

When you visit spairogram, we automatically collect certain information about your device and usage:

  • Cookies and Similar Technologies: We use cookies to remember your preferences, keep you logged in, and analyze traffic. See Section 4 for more details.

1.3 Information from Third Parties

We may receive information about you from third‑party services such as Google Analytics and our advertising partners. This information is aggregated and does not personally identify you. It helps us understand how users interact with our Site and improve your experience.
